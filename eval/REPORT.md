## Limitations

- The golden set is small and still `draft`; patient items were generated from the CSVs, guideline items were written by hand against the recommendation statements. Small differences are within noise.
- RAGAS covers a 20-item subset only, because of the free judge quota.
- Answer match is a strict substring check (dates accept common formats); a correct answer phrased differently can still miss.
- Only dates of birth are redacted; HIPAA Safe Harbor would also require generalising other dates.
